How to play

A secret colour code is hidden. Crack it before you run out of guesses.

  • Fill every slot with a colour, then press Guess. Colours can repeat.
  • The number beside your guess is how many pegs are in the correct position. Right colour in the wrong place does not count.
  • Here is the crooked part: it only gives you the count, never which pegs are right. You have to work that out.
  • Hint: tap it, then tap one of your placed pegs to learn whether that colour belongs in that slot (✓ or ✗). Hints are limited and do not use up a guess.
  • Time Attack: switch it on in the menu to play against the clock (). The clock pauses while this help is open.

Keyboard: 1–6 place a colour, Backspace erase, Enter guess, H hint, ← → move between slots.
